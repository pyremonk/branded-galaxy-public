# Branded Galaxy — First Five Turns Walkthrough

A turn-by-turn guide for each faction to help you find your footing during the early game.

> **Note:** UI instructions throughout the walkthrough tell you what to click in the in‑game interface. Example screenshots are referenced where helpful and assumed to live in a `screenshots/` folder next to this document.


---

## How a Turn Works

Before diving into faction strategies, here is the brief turn cycle:

1. **Action Phase** — Issue orders: move fleets, queue construction, start research, assign characters, launch missions.
2. **Resolution Phase** — All orders execute simultaneously: fleets move, combat resolves, buildings advance, missions return results.
3. **Income Phase** — Resources are collected from every system you control, modified by loyalty value and active bonuses.

Each turn produces credits, materials, recruits, and influence from your controlled systems. **Loyalty value** (0–100) on each system scales your income — keep it high.

## The Brand Authority

> *"Prosperity through order. Order through force."*

### Starting Conditions

- **Systems:** 12–15 (all high-population, pop 5–10)
- **Resources:** 2,000 credits · 1,500 materials · 500 recruits · 300 influence
- **Fleets:** 3 capital ships · 8 cruisers · 20 fighter squadrons
- **Starting Technology:** Military Logistics I (+10% fleet production speed)
- **Best at:** Overwhelming military force, economic output, territorial control
- **Watch out for:** Expensive research (+10% cost penalty), poor sabotage performance (-20%), high influence cost to flip enemy worlds (×2)

### Victory Paths (pick one to focus early)

| Condition | What It Takes |
|---|---|
| Territorial Control | 70% of all systems at loyalty ≥ 60 |
| Military Elimination | Destroy Union secret base + all Union/Autoforge fleets + Autoforge capitals |
| Economic Dominance | 500,000 credits + control 50% of trade hubs (requires *Galactic Administration* tech) |
| Discovery Victory | Locate and destroy the Union's hidden secret base |

**Recommended early focus:** Territorial Control — your large starting empire gives you a head start. Keep expanding while maintaining high loyalty across your core worlds.

---

### Turn-by-Turn Guide

#### Turn 1 — Establish the Economic Engine

**Research:** Queue **Corporate Tax Reform** (Tier 1 · 300 credits · 75 influence · 4 turns). Your +15% credit income bonus from faction traits stacks multiplicatively with this — it pays for itself quickly.

**Construction:** Identify your 2–3 highest-population systems. Start building a **Shipyard** at the largest one (costs 400 credits / 150 materials, completes in 3 turns). You need Shipyards at level 2+ before you can produce cruisers.

**Fleets:** Keep your 3 capital ships clustered together as a deterrent force. Leave cruisers patrolling your border systems. Do not overextend — you have the most systems and defending them matters more than capturing more right now.

**Characters:** Assign any Command-stat characters to your capital fleet group. This improves combat performance if the Autoforge or Union moves against you early.

> **Goal by end of Turn 1:** Research queued, first Shipyard started, fleets on patrol.

**UI Navigation:**
- **Research:** click the **flask** icon on the top bar to open the Research panel, select *Corporate Tax Reform* and hit **Queue**. A screenshot of the research panel is shown below:

  ![Research panel](screenshots/research-panel.png)

- **Construction:** click the desired system on the map, then click the **hammer** icon in the sidebar to open the Construction menu. Choose **Shipyard** and press **Build** (see screenshot).
- **Fleets:** select a fleet by clicking its ship icon on the map, then click or drag to a target system to move. The tooltip will display movement range and ETA.
- **Characters:** open the character roster by clicking the portrait button on the right edge of the screen, then drag your Command‑stat character onto the capital fleet icon to assign them.


---

#### Turn 2 — Scout and Secure Borders

**Research:** Corporate Tax Reform is ticking down. No new research needed yet.

**Construction:** Begin a **Training Facility** (300 credits / 100 materials, 2 turns) at a mid-border system. More recruits means more troops, which means harder to invade.

**Fleets:** Move a small cruiser detachment toward the nearest neutral high-population system. Neutral systems can be converted through diplomacy (influence) or simply occupied by moving a fleet there. Prefer diplomatic conversion — it avoids combat and builds loyalty faster.

**Missions:** If you have a character with Espionage ≥ 3, launch an **Espionage Mission** into Union territory. Early intel on their fleet positions and facilities is valuable. There is also a **5% per-turn passive chance** of discovering the Union's secret base — espionage stacks on top of this.

> **Goal by end of Turn 2:** First neutral system claimed or targeted, Training Facility queued, border patrols set.

**UI Navigation:**
- **Research:** (if any) use the flask icon as above.
- **Construction:** click the target system, hammer icon, select **Training Facility**, then **Build**.
- **Fleets:** select your cruiser squadron and click the neutral system to move; alternatively drag the fleet to the system to occupy it. If converting diplomatically, select the fleet and click the **diplomacy** button in the lower HUD when standing on the neutral world.
- **Missions:** open the Mission menu by clicking the **briefcase** icon on the right, choose your Espionage‑stat character, then click the white target icon on a Union system and select **Espionage Mission** from the popup.


---

#### Turn 3 — Expand and Research Combo

**Research:** Corporate Tax Reform completes this turn (if started on Turn 1 at 4 turns). Immediately queue **Advanced Materials Science** (Tier 1 · 250 credits · 60 influence · 3 turns). More materials means faster construction across the board.

**Construction:** Your first Shipyard finishes this turn. Begin a **second Shipyard** at a different high-pop system to give yourself two production queues. Also start building a **Research Facility** (500 credits / 200 materials, 3 turns) to offset your +10% research cost penalty.

**Fleets:** Build 2–3 cruisers from your new Shipyard. Keep adding to your main strike force.

**Loyalty:** Check the loyalty value on recently claimed neutral systems. If any are below 60, spend influence on a diplomatic stabilization action to push them above the threshold — systems below 60 produce at 70–85% efficiency and don't count toward Territorial Control victory progress.

> **Goal by end of Turn 3:** Two active Shipyards, Research Facility started, 1–2 new systems absorbed and stabilized.

**UI Navigation:**
- **Research:** use the flask icon to queue **Advanced Materials Science**.
- **Construction:** click each system where you want new buildings, then hammer → **Shipyard** or **Research Facility** → **Build**.
- **Fleets:** click the new Shipyard and press the **Produce Fleet** button to begin cruiser construction; you can also queue builds from the Production panel accessed via the docking system.
- **Loyalty:** click a recently captured system and then click the **diplomacy/stabilization** button (shield icon) to spend influence and raise loyalty; the loyalty meter will appear in the system info popup.


---

#### Turn 4 — Begin Military Projection

**Research:** Advanced Materials Science wraps up. Now unlock Tier 2: queue **Fleet Doctrine: Overwhelming Force** (Tier 2 · 600 credits · 150 influence · 5 turns). This adds +15% ship combat power — a meaningful edge in any fleet engagement.

**Construction:** Upgrade your primary Shipyard to **Level 2** (upgrade cost = base cost × 0.5, 2 turns) to unlock cruiser production slots. Start producing **cruisers** as your primary combat unit — cruisers are your best value ship at 35 combat power.

**Fleets:** If the Autoforge has lightly defended border systems, this is the first realistic opportunity for a punishing strike. Brand Authority's +20% fleet production advantage means you replace losses faster. A 2:1 cruiser advantage usually wins engagements decisively.

**Missions:** Push another Espionage mission into Union territory. If any Union character has been captured, assign an interrogation (50% chance of revealing secret base location). Finding the base early opens up the Discovery Victory path.

> **Goal by end of Turn 4:** Tier 2 research active, first offensive fleet assembled (3+ cruisers, 1 capital), Union intel gathering active.

**UI Navigation:**
- **Research:** open the flask menu and queue **Fleet Doctrine: Overwhelming Force**.
- **Construction/Upgrade:** select your primary Shipyard and click the **Upgrade** button; confirm to start the Level 2 upgrade. The upgrade progress bar appears in the building panel.
- **Fleets:** after producing cruisers, group them by shift‑clicking multiple units then right‑click the capital ship to form a combat group, or use the **Group** button in the fleet sidebar. Drag the group toward Autoforge border systems to prepare attacks.
- **Missions:** launch another espionage mission via the briefcase menu and select **Interrogation** if you have captured characters by clicking them in the character roster and choosing the interrogation action.


---

#### Turn 5 — Commit to a Victory Path

By Turn 5 you should have a clear read on how the game is shaping up. Choose your commit:

**Path A — Territorial Control:**
- Count how many systems are at loyalty ≥ 60. You need 70% of all systems.
- Build **Trade Centers** (350 credits / 100 materials, 2 turns) on neutral-adjacent systems to improve conversion efficiency (your +10% trade hub bonus applies).
- Keep diplomatic missions running on contested neutral systems.

**Path B — Military Elimination:**
- Move your main fleet group (capital + 5+ cruisers) toward the Autoforge capital cluster.
- Autoforge has few recruits — their ground defense is weak. Capture their capital systems first.
- Meanwhile, keep espionage running to locate the Union base.

**Research for Turn 5 forward:** After Fleet Doctrine, queue **Propaganda Networks** (Tier 2 · influence generation +20%) if going Territorial/Economic, or **Corporate Defense Grid** (Tier 3) for a Military path.

> **Goal by end of Turn 5:** Victory path locked, lead in systems and economic output established.

**UI Navigation:**
- **Systems & Loyalty:** open the Galaxy map via the map icon (lower left) to count high‑loyalty worlds; use the loyalty filters to highlight systems ≥60.
- **Construction:** build **Trade Centers** by clicking neutral‑adjacent systems → hammer icon → **Trade Center** → **Build**. Diplomatic missions can be launched from the same system info popup by clicking the diplomacy/mission button.
- **Fleets:** move your main strike fleet using the map as described earlier; if attacking the Autoforge capital cluster, right‑click their system to initiate combat.

*(Repeat analogous UI notes for research choices described above – use the Research panel and queue items as needed.)*

## The Union

> *"Any cost is justified when freedom is the prize."*

### Starting Conditions

- **Systems:** 8–10 (moderate population, pop 3–7)
- **Resources:** 1,000 credits · 800 materials · 600 recruits · **500 influence**
- **Fleets:** 1 capital ship · 5 cruisers · 15 fighter squadrons
- **Starting Technology:** Guerrilla Tactics I (+15% sabotage mission success)
- **Special:** **Secret Base** — a hidden installation (max pop 3, not adjacent to Brand Authority) invisible to enemies
- **Best at:** Espionage, loyalty subversion, low-cost fleet maintenance, influence generation
- **Watch out for:** Hard difficulty — smaller fleet (70% cap), fewer systems, -10% base resource production, and your one secret base is a critical vulnerability

### Victory Paths (pick one to focus early)

| Condition | What It Takes |
|---|---|
| Covert Liberation | Subvert a set number of Brand Authority systems to Union loyalty through espionage/diplomacy |
| Brand Authority Collapse | Destroy Brand Authority's economic infrastructure and eliminate their capital fleets |
| Diplomatic Unification | Achieve maximum influence in all galaxy sectors |
| Resistance Network | Establish active resistance cells (characters on mission) in every sector simultaneously |

**Recommended early focus:** Resistance Network / Covert Liberation — play to your faction strengths. Your starting 500 influence, +30% espionage bonus, and +25% loyalty gain make covert operations genuinely powerful.

---

### Turn-by-Turn Guide

#### Turn 1 — Build the Network

**Research:** Queue **Underground Networks** (Tier 1 · 250 credits · 75 influence · 3 turns). This improves diplomatic mission success by 20% and reduces all mission costs by 10% — a double payoff that accelerates everything you do.

**Secret Base:** Confirm your secret base location. Never move fleets through it — fleet movement is visible to opponents. Keep the base completely quiet and use it only to station characters between missions.

**Characters:** Assign your highest Espionage-stat character to an **Espionage Mission** against the nearest Brand Authority system. Even a failure attempt generates intelligence that helps you plan future operations; a success reveals their facilities and troop counts.

**Fleets:** Keep your single capital ship docked behind your deepest system. You cannot afford to lose it. Spread your cruisers across your 8–10 systems in pairs — one or two cruisers defending each system is enough to deter small raiding forces.

> **Goal by end of Turn 1:** Espionage mission launched, Underground Networks research ticking, secret base secure.

**UI Navigation:**
- **Research:** click the flask icon and queue **Underground Networks**.
- **Secret base:** hover over the system icon to reveal its name; the base is not selectable but shown on the mini‑map. Avoid moving fleets through it by keeping them in other systems.
- **Characters:** open the roster (portrait button), select your Espionage character, then click **Launch Mission** and choose a Brand Authority system from the galaxy map pop‑up.
- **Fleets:** click your capital ship icon, then click a defensive system to keep it there; use the movement arrow to rearrange cruisers across border worlds.


---

#### Turn 2 — Seeds of Resistance

**Research:** Underground Networks completes in 2 more turns. No additional research needed yet.

**Missions:** Your espionage mission returns results. Launch a **Diplomatic Mission** against the nearest neutral high-population system. Your +25% loyalty gain bonus means each successful diplomatic action is more effective than for other factions — you can flip loyalty faster with less influence spent.

**Construction:** Build a **Training Facility** at your highest-recruit system (300 credits / 100 materials, 2 turns). More recruits are essential — the Union's fleet size cap means you compensate with troops for ground defense.

**Fleets:** Do not initiate combat. If the Brand Authority moves against you, fall back rather than fight a pitched battle. Your lighter fleets and fleet maintenance bonus (-15%) let you sustain a war of attrition — but not a direct slugfest.

**Influence:** You start with 500 influence — more than either rival faction. Spend it aggressively on diplomatic missions against neutral systems rather than sitting on it. Influence generating below a loyalty threshold decays; put it to work.

> **Goal by end of Turn 2:** First neutral system diplomatically targeted, Training Facility started.

**UI Navigation:**
- **Research:** monitor the progress bar in the top bar next to the flask icon.
- **Missions:** open the briefcase menu, pick your Espionage character, click a neutral system on the map and select **Diplomatic Mission** from the choices.
- **Construction:** click your primary system, hammer icon, build **Training Facility**.
- **Fleets:** avoid combat by selecting cruisers and using the red retreat arrow if an enemy fleet approaches. You can also right‑click to move them back.
- **Influence:** click a system and then the **influence** button (star icon) in the system info to start spending influence.


---

#### Turn 3 — Exploiting Weaknesses

**Research:** Underground Networks completes (Turn 1 + 3 turns). Immediately queue **Popular Support** (Tier 2 · 600 credits · 150 influence · 5 turns). This reduces loyalty decay rates further and boosts influence generation — critical for sustaining a Diplomatic Unification or Covert Liberation victory.

**Missions:** Launch a **Sabotage Mission** against a Brand Authority shipyard or trade hub. Your Guerrilla Tactics I starting tech (+15% sabotage success) and +30% espionage faction bonus combine for a very high success rate. Destroying a Brand Authority shipyard sets back their fleet construction by 3 turns.

**Secret Base:** Station a second character at the secret base. Characters stationed at a base generate passive bonuses (engineering characters reduce build times, espionage characters generate intel). The base is your safest character haven.

**Territory:** If your first diplomatic efforts have converted a neutral system, start influence investment to push its loyalty above 60. Remember your loyalty decay penalty only applies to enemy conversions of *your* systems — your own loyal worlds are more resistant.

> **Goal by end of Turn 3:** Sabotage landed on Brand Authority infrastructure, one neutral system converted, Popular Support research queued.

**UI Navigation:**
- **Research:** queue **Popular Support** through the flask panel.
- **Missions:** launch a Sabotage mission via the briefcase icon; select a Brand Authority shipyard or trade hub and confirm the mission in the popup.
- **Secret base:** open the base menu by clicking its (hidden) system from the roster filter ‘Secret Base’ and drag a character into it. The base UI will show bonus icons.
- **Territory:** after a diplomatic success, click the conquered system then use the loyalty slider (shield icon) to invest influence.


---

#### Turn 4 — Grow the Resistance

**Research:** Popular Support is mid-research. Queue **Stealth Technology** (Tier 2 · 550 credits · 125 influence · 5 turns) in parallel if your research allows it — this adds +25% espionage success and unlocks stealth ships, the Union's most important fleet unit.

**Missions:** By Turn 4 the Autoforge is usually deep in their tech tree and lightly defended militarily (low recruits). A Sabotage Mission against an Autoforge manufacturing center can cripple their material production for several turns. Low-risk, high-reward.

**Fleets:** If you have accumulated enough materials and credits, build 1–2 additional cruisers. Cruisers cost 250 materials each and take 3 turns to produce — start now for Turn 7 reinforcements.

**Secret Base — Protect It:** The Brand Authority has a 15% per-turn espionage intercept chance of discovering your base, plus 50% chance if they interrogate one of your captured characters. **Never let a character on a Brand Authority-territory mission get captured.** If a mission shows high capture risk, abort it.

> **Goal by end of Turn 4:** At least 2 successful sabotage missions completed, 3+ systems under Union loyalty, characters safe.

**UI Navigation:**
- **Research:** open the flask panel and queue **Stealth Technology** if research slot is free; you can open a second slot with Research Acceleration if available.
- **Missions:** to send sabotage against the Autoforge factory, click a factory site on their map and choose **Sabotage Mission**; a success confirmation banner will appear after resolution.
- **Fleets:** build new cruisers by selecting a Shipyard and pressing **Produce**; check production queue in the building tooltip.
- **Secret base protection:** mouse over characters engaged in missions to see their capture risk; abort high‑risk missions using the **Cancel** button in the mission popup.


---

#### Turn 5 — Choose How You Win

**Path A — Covert Liberation:**
- Keep running Diplomatic + Espionage missions on Brand Authority systems. Each successfully subverted system weakens their resource income.
- With Popular Support active (if researched), loyalty shifts are amplified — you can cascade-convert neighboring systems once one flips.
- Target systems bordering the Brand Authority capital cluster for maximum disruption.

**Path B — Resistance Network:**
- Ensure you have characters on active missions in *every* galaxy sector simultaneously.
- This requires a large and well-managed character roster — assign characters from the secret base to missions systematically, rotating as they return.
- Upgrade your Training Facility to generate more recruit-based mission readiness.

**Path C — Brand Authority Collapse:**
- This is the hardest path but most satisfying. Keep sabotaging until Brand Authority's economy is in tatters, then coordinate a fleet engagement when their production base is crippled.
- Only pursue this if you've built up 7–8 cruisers and maintained your capital ship.

> **Goal by end of Turn 5:** Resistance cells active in 2+ sectors, Brand Authority infrastructure visibly damaged, secret base still undiscovered.

**UI Navigation:**
- **Missions:** use the briefcase menu to check all active missions; there is a tab for each sector so you can ensure one character is deployed per sector.
- **Damage assessment:** open the galaxy map and enable the **Infrastructure** overlay (button at top of map) to view Brand Authority shipyard/trade hub statuses.
- **Security:** to verify if your base has been detected, check the **intel** log via the envelope icon on the top bar; any discovery event will be listed.

## The Autoforge

> *"Every inefficiency must be eliminated. Every resource must be utilized."*

### Starting Conditions

- **Systems:** 6–8 (moderate population, pop 4–8)
- **Resources:** 1,500 credits · **3,000 materials** · 200 recruits · 200 influence
- **Fleets:** 2 capital ships · 6 cruisers · 12 fighter squadrons
- **Starting Technology:** Automated Production I (+20% material production)
- **Special:** All starting systems begin with a **Fabrication Plant** (+25% manufacturing output)
- **Best at:** Materials production, research speed, zero-maintenance facilities, technical dominance
- **Watch out for:** Very few recruits (-50% generation means half the troops), expensive and ineffective diplomacy (×2 cost), no loyalty mechanics (can't use influence-based loyalty plays), organic characters banned

### Victory Paths (pick one to focus early)

| Condition | What It Takes |
|---|---|
| Total Optimization | Convert all controlled systems to maximum manufacturing efficiency |
| Technological Singularity | Research all Tier 4 and Tier 5 technologies |
| Galactic Integration | Absorb every system into the Autoforge network (capture/convert all systems) |
| Industrial Supremacy | Own the highest total materials production in the galaxy by a large margin |

**Recommended early focus:** Industrial Supremacy / Technological Singularity — your +40% resource production and +20% research speed make you the fastest faction to tech up. Build the machine, let it run.

---

### Turn-by-Turn Guide

#### Turn 1 — Accelerate the Tech Tree

**Research:** Queue **Neural Network Expansion** (Tier 1 · 250 credits · 60 influence · 3 turns). Your +20% research speed faction bonus reduces effective research turns. This tech adds another +15% research speed — the compounding effect means Tier 3 techs that take 8 turns for the Brand Authority take you 5–6 turns.

**Construction:** Build a **Research Facility** (500 credits / 200 materials, 3 turns) at your highest-population system. Your +40% production bonus and starting Fabrication Plants give you ample materials to fund construction aggressively. Research Facilities give -10% tech research cost — critical for fueling your tech rush.

**Fleets:** Station both capital ships at your two most valuable systems (highest pop / highest materials output). You have fewer systems than the Brand Authority and cannot afford to lose a core world in Turn 1–2.

**Note on Recruits:** With only 200 starting recruits and -50% generation, every recruit is precious. Do not waste them on fighter crew. Conserve recruits for emergency ground defense.

> **Goal by end of Turn 1:** Neural Network Expansion research queued, Research Facility started.

**UI Navigation:**
- **Research:** click the flask icon and queue **Neural Network Expansion**. The research panel shows your +20% speed bonus at the top.
- **Construction:** select your highest‑population system, then click the hammer icon and choose **Research Facility** → **Build**. The build progress bar appears on the system tile.
- **Fleets:** click each capital ship icon and drag them to your two most valuable systems; use the info tooltip to confirm system value.


---

#### Turn 2 — Parallel Construction

**Research:** Neural Network Expansion is ticking. No change needed.

**Construction:** With 3,000 starting materials (the most of any faction), you can afford to build in multiple systems simultaneously. Start a **Shipyard** (400 credits / 150 materials, 3 turns) at a second system in addition to the Research Facility. Your zero-maintenance facility bonus means both buildings cost you nothing per turn once completed.

**Fleets:** Build 3–4 **Fighter Squadrons** from your existing Shipyards (100 credits / 20 materials, 1 turn each). Fighters are cheap and fast — they compensate for your low troop count in defense. Also begin **Drone Fleet Protocols** research once Neural Network finishes — this further reduces fleet maintenance cost and boosts fighter production speed.

**Expansion:** Identify the 1–2 nearest neutral systems. Unlike the Union (which uses diplomacy) and Brand Authority (which uses military), the Autoforge expands most efficiently by **moving a fleet into an uncontested neutral system**. Since loyalty mechanics don't apply to you, system function is purely about production output.

> **Goal by end of Turn 2:** Shipyard started at second system, Fighter production running, expansion target identified.

**UI Navigation:**
- **Construction:** click your second system → hammer icon → **Shipyard** → **Build**. Use the system popup to verify the second build queue.
- **Production:** click an existing Shipyard to open its production panel, then queue multiple **Fighter Squadron** units. The queue list appears on the right side of the building info.
- **Expansion:** select a fleet and right‑click a neutral system to move; you can also use the **Map** overlay (map icon) to pick the system before issuing the move order.


---

#### Turn 3 — The Production Engine Online

**Research:** Neural Network Expansion completes this turn (+15% research speed, stacking with your faction's +20%). Immediately queue **Research Acceleration** (Tier 2 · 700 credits · 150 influence · 6 turns — but effectively 4–5 turns for you). This unlocks **2 research slots simultaneously** — the single most powerful advantage the Autoforge can achieve early.

**Construction:** Your Research Facility completes. Now also start **Advanced Fabrication** (Tier 2 research, queued after Research Acceleration in priority). Once ready, this +30% material production effect pushes your per-turn materials income to extraordinary levels.

**Fleets:** Upgrade your primary Shipyard to Level 2 to unlock Cruiser production. Begin building 2 cruisers per turn from your now Level 2 Shipyard (3 turn build time, but faster with your production bonuses).

**Systems Gained:** You should have 1–2 additional systems absorbed by now. Each new Autoforge system inherited from the galaxy generator starts benefiting from your production bonuses immediately — there is no loyalty ramp-up period for you.

> **Goal by end of Turn 3:** Research Acceleration queued, 8+ systems, Shipyard at Level 2.

**UI Navigation:**
- **Research:** add **Research Acceleration** via the flask panel; you can open a second slot by clicking the **+** button in the research panel once the tech completes.
- **Construction:** click a recently acquired system and build additional Shipyards or Research Facilities as needed using the hammer menu.
- **Fleets:** upgrade your primary Shipyard by selecting it and clicking **Upgrade**; a confirmation dialog shows the cost and time.


---

#### Turn 4 — Dual Research Unlocked

**Research:** Research Acceleration completes (or nearly so). The moment you have **2 research slots**, run:
- Slot 1: **Advanced Fabrication** (if not already done) — +30% material production + unlocks automated-factory building
- Slot 2: **Droid Legion** (Tier 2 · recruit generation ×2, unlocks Droid Carrier unit) — your biggest weakness is recruits; this partially compensates

**Construction:** Begin building **Automated Factories** (unlocked once Advanced Fabrication researches) at every system. These facilities + your Fabrication Plants + the +40% faction bonus creates a material surplus that bankrolls an unstoppable fleet build.

**Fleets:** Push fleet count to 10+ cruisers. You don't pay maintenance (faction bonus: 0% facility maintenance applies to ships too via the Drone Fleet Protocols tech chain). A large, free-to-maintain fleet is how you convert your production advantage into map control.

**Threat Assessment:** The Brand Authority at Turn 4 may have 15+ systems and a large fleet. Do not fight them head-on yet. Raid their resource systems to slow their construction timelines; retreat if they bring a capital ship.

> **Goal by end of Turn 4:** 2 active research slots, Automated Factories under construction, cruiser fleet at 8–10 ships.

**UI Navigation:**
- **Research slots:** open the research panel (flask) to confirm two active slots; you can drag new techs into either slot directly.
- **Construction:** click each system and use the hammer menu to place **Automated Factory** builds; the icon appears under Tier 2 constructions once unlocked.
- **Fleets:** select your upgraded Shipyard and queue 2‑3 cruisers per turn via the production panel; monitor the build timer on the Shipyard tooltip.


---

#### Turn 5 — Optimization Path

By Turn 5, the Autoforge's compounding advantages should be pulling significantly ahead of rivals in materials and research progress. Commit to your victory path:

**Path A — Industrial Supremacy:**
- Maximize manufacturing output on every system (Fabrication Plant + Automated Factory + Manufacturing Center location bonus if applicable).
- You need the *highest total materials production in the galaxy by a large margin*. With your bonuses, this is achievable if you keep expanding and building.
- Research **Hyper-Manufacturing** (Tier 3) as your next target to extend this lead.

**Path B — Technological Singularity:**
- With dual research slots, calculate the number of turns to research all Tier 4–5 techs.
- Tier 5 techs cost 4,000–6,000 credits + 800–1,200 influence each. Start accumulating influence now — buy Research Facilities at every captured system to reduce costs.
- This is the fastest victory path if enemies don't contest you militarily.

**Path C — Galactic Integration:**
- Use your superior fleet to systematically capture remaining neutral systems and push into enemy territories.
- The Autoforge faces double diplomatic costs, so conquest is your only viable expansion method.
- Priority target: Brand Authority trade hubs (destroys their credit income, weakening their fleet replacement capacity).

**Research Turn 5 forward:** After your dual-slot is active, the priority queue is:
1. Advanced Fabrication → Automated Factories
2. Droid Legion → more recruits
3. Hyper-Manufacturing → Industrial Supremacy path
4. Singularity Protocol (Tier 4) → Technological Singularity path

> **Goal by end of Turn 5:** 10–12 systems, materials income dominant, victory path chosen and tech chain locked in.

**UI Navigation:**
- **System count:** open the galaxy map and use the system counter in the top‑right to verify you control 10–12 systems; the map overlay shows your color.
- **Production optimization:** to maximize material output, click each system and check that **Automated Factory** and **Fabrication Plant** icons are visible; the tooltip will display the current material rate.
- **Research path:** use the research panel to queue follow‑up techs. Drag them from the tech tree into your queue or click the **Queue** button on their info card.

*(For whichever victory path you choose, navigate through the same UI panels described above — construction via hammer, fleets via map selection, research via flask.)*

## Cross-Faction Tips

- **Don't fight the Brand Authority directly in Turns 1–3** unless you have a strong numerical advantage. Their +20% fleet production and high credit income let them replace losses faster than anyone.
- **The Union's secret base is a game-end threat.** If you're Brand Authority, dedicate at least one espionage character to hunting it from Turn 2 onward. If you're the Union, never route active mission characters back through their base system.
- **Autoforge expand by moving fleets, not spending influence**. If you're fighting the Autoforge and they're quiet — check the map. They're almost certainly absorbing neutral systems while you're distracted.
- **Loyalty above 60 is the efficiency threshold.** All factions benefit from pushing system loyalty above 60 (full 85–100% resource multiplier). Don't spread yourself thin claiming systems you can't hold at high loyalty.
- **Research Facilities reduce costs by 10–20% per level.** All factions benefit from building these early, but it's especially powerful for the Brand Authority (who has a +10% research cost penalty) and the Autoforge (who pairs it with their research speed bonus).
